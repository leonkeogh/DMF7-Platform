How It Works

Four steps from input to result

01

Enqueue

Submit tasks with a payload. The engine accepts work at any scale without dropping requests.

02

Assign

Workers claim tasks atomically. No duplicates, no races — exactly one worker per task.

03

Execute

Workers process their assigned task. Stale assignments are automatically reclaimed.

04

Validate

Results are verified and recorded. Failed tasks retry once. Outcomes are permanent.
